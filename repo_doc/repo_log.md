- 2026-08-21 20:00-21:30: **Mocap verified, the arm calibrated, and a defect found in the forward kinematics** - the 2000-2005 rigid-body block is confirmed live (census: 500-505 RS485, 1008 Kinova, 2000-2005 this arm, four labeled markers each at 115 Hz), the actuator/axis map is measured for all 24 boards, and `fkine` reproduces the measured u-joint centres to **1.99 mm RMS / 7.0 mm worst on 18 held-out multi-joint poses**, from 109 mm with what the repo shipped. Three findings and one fix:
  - **The marker arms lie ALONG the revolute axes, not 45 deg from them.** Driving each antagonistic pair alone put the four proximal drive axes at 45/135/225/315 deg in the marker-diagonal frame, mutually orthogonal to 0.68-1.36 deg - a 45 deg frame error, which yields a smooth, repeatable, wrong `q`. `UMArm_MOCAP/canarm_frames.py` carries the measured per-plate azimuth; two independent estimates (drive axes + co-rigidity, and a refit against predicted centres) agree to 0.82 deg. Co-rigid plate pairs 1/2 and 3/4 agree to **0.00 deg +- 0.038** over 68 poses, which validates the whole marker-only construction without fitting anything.
  - **Segment 1's top regulator platform is wired a quarter turn round.** Segments 2 and 3 reproduce the research tree's table exactly, all sixteen boards including sign (which also pins the sign convention); segment 1 is the same four antagonistic pairs each driving the *other* axis of its universal joint, with the signs a +90 deg rotation implies. `UMArm_KINEMATICS/canarm_actuators.py` holds the measurement and the legacy claim side by side.
  - **`fkine` composed the proximal universal joint's two twists in the wrong order.** The residual rotation the two-angle distal reader silently drops was -0.91 to -0.95 times `t1*t2` across all three segments (residual sd 0.09-0.25 deg against a raw spread of 1.0-1.4 deg) - the exact non-commutativity signature of a swapped pair. `fkine` now takes `order`; the default is bit-identical to the Mathematica oracle and the CAN arm uses `"yx"`, which took the held-out error from 4.64 mm to 1.99 mm.
  - **Lengths measured**: the five u-joint centre gaps are 265.36 / 72.89 / 234.37 / 72.99 / 229.92 mm (sd 0.03-0.47 mm over 68 poses), within 0.04-1.8 mm of the research tree's CAN table - so that table was right and the RS485-scaled placeholder this repo carried was wrong by 46-182 mm. `canarm_params.MEASURED` is now `True`.
